## ★ THE FLAGSHIP HOOK, VERBATIM — and the first time it has ever run as a video
Hook: **"The newborn moments nobody tells you you'll miss…"** — Ben's all-time best hook
([[drift-hero-hook]]). Wording is kept **verbatim, ellipsis included**.

⚠️ **This breaks `reel-copy-spec.md` §3's "drop the ellipsis-tease" rule, on purpose.** The verbatim
instruction in [[drift-hero-hook]] is the stronger claim, and the ellipsis is part of what is being
re-run. Recorded rather than silently resolved.

⚠️ **It also breaks `lane-mix-bands.md`'s line that the flagship hero hook can never take an AM row.**
That line was written when AM-reel meant the hook's carousel layout could not fit. The hero hook is a
four-item moments inventory — it fits the reel card exactly. And the reason to do it is the single
best-evidenced fact in the system: `card-patterns.md` records the **same hook** running as carousel
post 30 (715 views) and as REEL 03 (**21,440 views**, 21.88 shares/1k). ER barely moved; the container
was everything. The account's best hook has never been put in the account's best container.

**Cadence.** Third outing was Sep 6 (423 views, reach_x 0.83, but **19.3s watch — the highest number
on the account**: distribution fatigue, not copy fatigue). It re-aired Sep 10 as post 68. Thu Oct 1 is
**21 days** after that — exactly the 3-week floor `hook-bank.md` set when it corrected
[[drift-hero-hook]]'s "every 1-2 weeks" against first-party data. Not a day sooner.

**CTA is Share, not the website ask, and that is the experiment's hygiene.** A website card would add
a second variable to a run whose whole question is *what does this hook do as a reel*. It also keeps
the number comparable to REEL 19 and REEL 24, the two share winners of the week of Sep 14, both on
growth CTAs. Every Keepsake reel that has carried the website ask has returned **zero shares**
(REEL 22: 2,572 views, 0 shares).

**Items are all new** — the hook has run three times as a carousel and none of these four have
appeared under it. The twist held throughout: every item is a thing she is currently enduring.
