How to stay present this month with your newborn.

Pick one moment a day and don't reach for your phone. Say out loud the thing you want to keep. Put one photo in a folder called “this month,” notice the sound before you notice the mess, and let one thing on the list stay unfinished.

Save this and try one tomorrow. 🤍
