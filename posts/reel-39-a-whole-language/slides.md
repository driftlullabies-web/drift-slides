## 🟢 THIS ROW EXISTS BECAUSE BEN CUT 86 — and it is deliberately a REEL in the MID slot

Thursday's Mid carousel (**86 · You Dont Have To Be A Songwriter**) was cut on review. Two decisions
went into the replacement.

**1. It is not another lullaby-subject post.** He cut *both* of the week's product-subject posts (86
and the 88 bench), so refilling the slot with a third would be answering the judgement rather than
hearing it. The replacement comes from **Inspired**, the lane he named in the same review:
*"when you are creative on your own, like in the inspired category, you do really well."*

**2. It is a REEL, and that is the week's one structural experiment.** The 2026-09-21 read found the
Mid slot unreadable for a reason that had nothing to do with the hour:

> *Under the current day shape **every AM post is a reel and every Mid post is a carousel**, so
> "Mid is weak" and "carousels are weak" are the same observation counted twice. To get a real
> reading somebody has to put a reel in Mid, or a carousel in AM.*

Nobody had. **This is that post.** Every Mid row for two months has landed in a 231–336 view band
while AM reels took 88% of the week's views, and until a reel runs at 2pm there is no way to tell
which of the two facts is doing the work. One card settles a confound that three weeks of lane
averages could not.

**Read it against REEL 32 / 34 / 36** — same lane, same week, same container, AM — not against the
Mid carousels.

## Mechanism: witness, pointed at competence rather than at labour
Written natively, not adapted. The three scrape-sourced Inspired rows this week are about what goes
**unseen** (REEL 32), **unforgiven** (REEL 34) and **unspoken** (REEL 36). This one is about what
goes **uncredited**, which is a fourth distinct pain moment — she is good at something difficult and
there is no institution that says so.

Checked at 3d against all three: no shared pain moment, no shared noun, no item overlap.
