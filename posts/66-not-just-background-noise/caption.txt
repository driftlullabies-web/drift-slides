A lullaby isn't just background noise to your newborn.

White noise masks the room — a melody gives them something to follow. A song has a shape: the same rise and fall, in the same order, every night, and that repeated shape can become a cue their body starts to expect. It travels with them too — the car, a grandparent's house, a hotel room. And a sound machine will never say their name.
