The little weekly traditions you'll be glad you started.

Bake something together every Sunday — make one for your family, and one to give away. Pizza on Friday, eaten outside, even in October. A quick walk after dinner, same route, every night. Saturday morning at the farmers market. One day a week with every screen in the house off. And always a lullaby before bed — a familiar nightcap to every day.

Start one now and they'll grow up thinking it's just what your family does.

Which one would you actually keep? 🤍
