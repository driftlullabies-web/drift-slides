The traditions you'll wish you'd started in your baby's first year.

A letter written to them each December, sealed. The same questions asked every birthday, so you can watch the answers change. A charm added to their bracelet every birthday, a book bought and inscribed for them each year, and the outfit they came home in, kept and framed the year they turn one. None of it takes long. All of it stacks.

What's one from your family? 🤍
