The traditions you'll wish you'd started in your baby's first year.

A letter written to them each December, sealed. The same questions asked every birthday, so you can watch the answers change. One jar, one folded note a week, opened at New Year's. A photo in the same doorway. A minute of video on the same date every year. None of it takes long. All of it stacks.
