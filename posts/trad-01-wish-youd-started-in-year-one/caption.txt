The traditions you'll wish you'd started in your baby's first year.

A letter written to them each December, sealed and saved for their eighteenth. The same questions asked every birthday, so you can watch the answers change. A charm added to their bracelet every year. The same orchard every autumn, with whatever you pick baked that weekend — and their winter coat from the same little shop on Main Street, every year they outgrow the last.

None of it takes long. All of it stacks.

What's one from your family? 🤍
