## REVISION 2 — 2026-08-16, second review pass. Read this before touching the lane again.

Ben's note: *"I feel like the posts claude was coming up with when making this lane were way better
than this. Why is it missing the mark??? It's a lot of birthday. It's just not great writing/ideas
for traditions. Things like baking, shopping local, etc. are missing here."*

He deleted items 04 and 05 in the spreadsheet and rewrote 02 and 03 in place. He was right, and the
count makes it obvious: **four of the five items hung off a birthday** (interview · charm every
birthday · book every birthday · framed the year they turn one). Five distinct items, one occasion.
From a scroller's side that is not a list, it's the same slide five times.

### Why it happened — three causes, all worth fixing above this file

1. **The engine tag was read as a constraint on items.** D2 was filed in the pool under *Engine 4 ·
   annual documentation*, so the restock only looked for annual-documentation-shaped items. But the
   hook itself — *"the traditions you'll wish you'd started in year one"* — is a **wishlist**, and a
   wishlist spans all four engines. The engine tag is a planning label for spreading a week's posts
   around; it is not a filter on what may appear inside one post.

2. **The item-ledger rule starved the restock.** Every item may appear exactly once across the pool
   (added Aug 15, for good reason — it killed a real broken-record problem). But by the time D2
   needed refilling, the strong weekly-rhythm and outing material was already spoken for by W1–W4,
   and the "unspent material" list that remained skewed annual and birthday-ish. The rule protected
   against repetition *across* posts and had nothing to say about monotony *within* one.

3. **Nothing checked occasion variety.** The pre-ship checklist counts hashtags, premises and
   absolutes. Nothing counted how many items share an anchor.

### The rule that comes out of this

> **Count the occasions, not the items.** Five items hanging off one anchor — one holiday, one
> birthday, one season, one room — is one item shown five times. **No more than two items in a post
> may share an occasion anchor**, and a five-item post should span at least three different rhythms
> (weekly · seasonal · annual · milestone). This is the item-level sibling of the Aug 9 keepsake
> lesson (*count premises, not hooks*) and the Aug 15 pool lesson (*count items, not hooks*).

### What changed here

Items 02 and 03 are **Ben's own words, verbatim** from his spreadsheet cells — note that his edit to
03 quietly did the real work, swapping *"every birthday"* for *"every year"* and de-anchoring it.
Items 01, 04 and 05 are new. The anchors now run **December · birthday · every year · autumn ·
whenever they outgrow a coat** — five rhythms, one birthday item instead of four. Items 04 and 05
are the baking and shop-local material Ben named as missing, written as outings with a rule in each
(§2 of the brief: *a tradition item needs a rule in it*), and both are startable in year one, which
is the test that killed a "back-to-school shoes" draft.

**Retired, not returned to the pool:** the inscribed birthday book and the framed homecoming outfit.
Both are decent items that failed only by being the third and fourth birthday in a row — but D2 has
now been restocked twice and should not be drawn from again. If a future hook wants them, it has to
earn them in a post whose other items are not birthdays.
