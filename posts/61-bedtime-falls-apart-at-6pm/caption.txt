What to do when your newborn's bedtime falls apart at 6pm.

Nothing is broken — early evening is often when newborns are hardest to settle. Shorten the routine instead of starting it over from the top, dim the room and drop your voice before you try again, and moving to a different room can break the loop for both of you. If it takes an hour tonight, that's still a bedtime, not a failure.

Save this for the next evening that goes sideways. 🤍
