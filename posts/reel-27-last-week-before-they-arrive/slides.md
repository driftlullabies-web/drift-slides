## ✅ REVISED 2026-09-17 per Ben's review — his wording, applied verbatim, and it is better

He rewrote **all four items and added a fifth**, then asked: *"Can we do 5 bullet points since I
made some shorter?"* **Yes — rendered at five and it is the stronger card.**

| | His | Mine |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The last time they're completely yours to carry. | The last time they're only yours, felt by no one |
| 2 | The last night you fall asleep wondering whose face they'll have. | …guessing whose nose they'll have |
| 3 | The last 2am kick under your ribs. | …one you'd have stayed up for |
| 4 | The last bump photo. | …the one you'd have taken slower if you'd known |
| 5 | **The last time you look down and realize they're still completely unknown to you.** | *(did not exist)* |

**Why five works here and the four-item house shape did not have to fight it.** The shape rule wants
item lengths to vary by ≥15 characters so the right edge stays ragged; his set runs **20 → 80
characters**, a spread of 60, because he cut items 3 and 4 to almost nothing. That gives the card a
real cadence — long, long, **short, short**, long — which four evenly-sized items never had. Rendered
at 34px, every item wraps to two lines or fewer, and the card comes in at 67 words, inside budget.

⚠️ **One consequence, flagged and not changed.** Pattern **P1** asks that two of the items carry a
*counterfactual regret* (*"the only one you'd have savoured if you had known"*) — that is one of the
four things `card-patterns.md` credits for REEL 03's 21.9 shares/1k. His trims removed both of them.
His item 5 does something different and arguably better: it is not a regret, it is the strangeness of
the moment itself. **This is a taste call on a proven pattern, so it is recorded rather than argued**
— and it is worth reading REEL 27's share rate specifically against REEL 03's, since the variable is
now isolated.
