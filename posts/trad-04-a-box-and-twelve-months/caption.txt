Family traditions that are really just a box and twelve months.

A shoebox a year: a drawing, a photo, that day's front page. Everyone writes a letter, sealed, opened next birthday. The list of their favorites, written down before it changes. A few cards kept back and hung as a banner. One ordinary day filmed once a year, never edited. Nothing to buy, nothing to keep up with.

What would go in yours? 🤍
