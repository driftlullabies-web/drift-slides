## Caption
New-parent fear, and why it turns up even when nothing is wrong.

It tends to scale with how much is at stake rather than with how likely anything is. Knowing that does not switch it off, but it can stop it feeling like a personal failing.

Send this to someone who checked twice tonight. 🤍
