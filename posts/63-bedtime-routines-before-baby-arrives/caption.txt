What to know about newborn bedtime routines before your baby arrives.

You won't need one for the first few weeks — newborns don't have a bedtime yet. When you do start, three or four cues in the same order is plenty, and it tends to work because it repeats, not because of what's in it. Whoever is holding the baby can run it. You can start at any age, and starting later costs you nothing.
