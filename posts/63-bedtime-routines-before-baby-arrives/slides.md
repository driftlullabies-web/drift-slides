## Cover
What you should know about bedtime routines before your baby arrives:

## Items
01  You won't need one for the first few weeks — newborns don't have a bedtime yet
02  When you do start, three or four cues in the same order is plenty
03  It tends to work because it repeats, not because of what's in it
04  Whoever is holding the baby can run it — it doesn't have to be you
05  You can start at any age, and starting later costs you nothing
