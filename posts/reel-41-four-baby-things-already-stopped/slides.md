## ✅ REVISED 2026-09-25 per Ben's review — his wording, applied verbatim

| | His | Mine |
|---|---|---|
| **cover** | Four things that can happen for the last time before they turn one | By their first birthday, four baby things have already stopped |

**His line hedges the claim and keeps the ambush.** Mine asserted that four things *have already*
stopped, which is a statement about her baby that may simply not be true yet — a one-year-old who is
still bottle-fed makes the card wrong on slide one. His *"can happen"* is the same
[[drift-claim-softening]] move the physiological lines get, applied to a timing claim, and it costs
the card nothing: *"before they turn one"* still does the work.

It also puts the countable promise and the deadline in the same clause, so the reader gets the frame
in one pass rather than two.

⚠️ **It is 13 words against the reel spec's 8–12 headline budget**, and it is not being trimmed —
`reel-copy-spec.md` §3 is a guideline and [[drift-autonomy-boundaries]] is not: never rewrite his
wording to satisfy a linter. Re-rendered and checked: the card still fits, all four items still wrap
to two lines, and the word count moved 63 → 66, inside the 45–70 target.
