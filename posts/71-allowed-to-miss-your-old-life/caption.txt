New mom, you're allowed to miss your old life and love your new one.

Missing sleeping in doesn't mean you regret them. Missing your old job, your old body, your old freedom — none of it cancels out how much you love this one. You can grieve the life you had and still not trade this one for it.

Two things can be true in the same breath. Both of them are allowed. 🤍
