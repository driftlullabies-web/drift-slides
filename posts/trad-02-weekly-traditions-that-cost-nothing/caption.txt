5 weekly family traditions that cost almost nothing.

A different playground every Saturday until you run out. One phone call to a grandparent, same day every week. Grocery-store flowers every Friday, whatever's cheapest. Dinner on a blanket on the floor, once a week. Mail a handwritten (or colored) letter together every Monday. Pick one and let it repeat — that's the whole thing.
