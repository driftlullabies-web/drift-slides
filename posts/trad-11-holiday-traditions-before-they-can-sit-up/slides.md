## Items
1 Walk the block the first night the neighbors' lights go up. Stroller, mittens, nowhere to be.
2 Pick one holiday market and go on its opening weekend. Same stall, every year.
3 Every Sunday in December, the same walk to the same bakery for one holiday treat.
4 Once a year, pick out a toy for a child their age and drop it off together.
5 Count down to New Year's at 7pm with a city where it's already midnight.
