I stopped worrying about newborn wake windows after learning this.

The numbers you found online are averages, not a plan your baby agreed to. Two babies the same age can want very different days, and ten minutes past a "window" rarely decides how the night goes. A window you miss is information, not a mistake you have to fix — use them as a rough shape and let tonight fill in the rest.

Save this for the next time the numbers stress you out. 🤍
