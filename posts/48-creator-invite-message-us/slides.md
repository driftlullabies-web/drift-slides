### Deviations from the locked brief, deliberate and approved

1. **Hook/bridge pairing.** §6 pairs a brand-deal hook with "Here's an easy first one:" and a gifted
   hook with "Here's the gift:". This post runs a **brand-deal hook against the gifted bridge** —
   Ben's pick, made knowingly. Consequence: its lead type is ambiguous, so the ledger logs it as its
   own lead (**C**) rather than filing it under brand deal or gifted.
2. **Six slides, not five.** §6 caps the series at five. The support slide is the sixth. Approved as
   an amendment rather than a one-off — see brief §6b.
3. **"Reaction video" is no longer the required noun.** §10 asks slide 3 to name it a "reaction
   video" for search clarity. Slide 4 here keeps the reaction as the lead example but permits
   "whatever feels natural," which lowers friction at the cost of a wider mix of footage coming back.
4. **Slide 2 says "just for your little one"**, not §10's "made just for you and your little one."
   Kept because it reads more them-focused.
