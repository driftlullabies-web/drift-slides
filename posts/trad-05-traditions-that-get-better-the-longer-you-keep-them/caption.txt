The traditions that only get better the longer you keep them.

One ornament a year, the year written on the back. Their height on the same doorframe every birthday. Their handwriting from each year, added to a scrapbook. Something planted on their birthday, watched every year after.

None of these matter much the first time. They start to mean something around year five. Which one's already yours? 🤍
