## Card (see card.json for the machine-readable source)
headline: The weird little things newborns do when they're getting sleepy
items:
  - The 47-position head bob before finally giving up and falling asleep
  - A stare that goes soft and unfocused, like they're looking past you
  - Fists that were tight all day, quietly starting to loosen
  - A yawn that isn't a yawn, more a full-body sigh
turn: The quiet one is usually the window. The crying comes later.

## Caption
Newborn sleepy cues are easier to read than most sleep advice makes them sound.

A lot of what gets called overtired is a window that closed about ten minutes earlier. Watching the body usually beats watching the clock.

Save this for the next stretch you're guessing through. 🤍
