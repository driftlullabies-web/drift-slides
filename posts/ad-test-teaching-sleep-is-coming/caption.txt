How to teach your newborn's brain that sleep is coming.

Most parents focus on getting their baby to sleep. They rarely think about teaching their baby's brain that sleep is coming. Babies learn through repetition long before they understand words, so the same song, phrase, or routine each night becomes a familiar signal — we're winding down, you're safe, sleep is coming. The goal isn't a perfect routine. It's a moment your little one learns to recognize.
