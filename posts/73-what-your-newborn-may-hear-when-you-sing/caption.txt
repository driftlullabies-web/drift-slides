What your newborn may hear when you sing to them — more than you'd think.

Newborns often respond to tone and pitch before they understand words. They can pick up on something that sounds familiar faster than you might realize. And a familiar voice often stands out to them, even in a noisy room.

Any lullaby can carry a tone they know. But imagine one written from the exact things you'd want them to hear — link in bio. 🤍
