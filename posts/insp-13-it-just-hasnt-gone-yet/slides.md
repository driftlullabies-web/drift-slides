⚠️ BACKGROUND — read before rebuilding or deriving anything from this post.
Poem-shape posts in this lane render on the **Lullaby-poetry photo background**, not a flat
destination colour — that is `render_poem_slide(photo=True)`, the default since Ben's 2026-08-27
standardisation (and the mist re-bake of 2026-08-30). The `destination` field below still governs
the COPY rule (which of lullaby/voice, newborn-general, mom-general the piece belongs to), but it
no longer picks a background for poems; the flat destination colours now apply only to the
Reframe/strikethrough treatment.

The Format line names `bg mist #DCE6EC` deliberately so `bg_for_slides_md()` resolves to the
closest flat equivalent of the photo (it is ~85% flat #D6DFE7 grey-blue) instead of raising or
landing on cream. Any derived Story teaser / IG reel built flat will therefore be in the right
colour family — but the right fix is to derive it on the photo background, same as the post.


Source: @the.3am.club "One day they'll sleep through the night… But not tonight." — 410K views,
64.8x median. A litany of futures, then a hard pivot back into the present.

⚠️ NEVER-LIFT: the source's hinge is the phrase "But not tonight." Changed to "None of that is
tonight" — three words is short enough that reusing it verbatim would read as lifted.

WHY IT ISN'T A FOURTH HELPING: REEL 03 and INSP 01 both work "an ending you don't see coming" —
the vein that produced the account's two best posts ever. This one refuses the future entirely and
stands in the room. Same engine, opposite direction, so it reads as a different experience rather
than more of the same.

ADJACENCY FLAGGED: post 43 "stay present this month" works a similar instinct, but that was
mid-August and it is an item card; this is a poem that spends a whole slide on four inches of
chest. Closest call in the batch; judged not close enough to matter.
