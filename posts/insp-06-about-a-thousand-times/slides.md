## Caption
Nobody counts this one. It happens about a thousand times before she turns two.

You might not realize how much it means to you: to sing her to sleep every night — a lullaby, or just your humming, a song that's holding you both.

And then one day, she falls asleep without it. You won't know it's the last one. So sing tonight's like it counts — one of them does. 🤍
