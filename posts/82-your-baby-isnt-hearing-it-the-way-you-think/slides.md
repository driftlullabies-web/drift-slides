## Cover
Your baby isn't hearing your lullaby the way you think they are:

## Items
1 They likely can't follow the words yet — it's your voice and rhythm doing the work, not the lyrics
2 A lullaby doesn't have to be "good." It just has to be yours, sung enough times to feel familiar
3 Singing it a little differently every night can still feel steady to them — it's the return that matters, not the performance

## Caption
What your baby is actually picking up when you sing to them.

Which is oddly freeing. None of it is a reason to stop singing badly — it's a reason to stop worrying about singing badly.
