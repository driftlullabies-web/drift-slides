## Card (see card.json — the source of truth)

It starts in the rear-view mirror. It never really stops.

You're three weeks old, rear-facing.
I check the mirror at every light.

You're six and kicking my seat.
I check the mirror. You make a face.

You're seventeen and driving.
I watch the dot until it's home.

You're thirty, buckling in your own.
I check the straps twice. You let me.

*The mirror changes every few years.*
*I never stop looking back.*
