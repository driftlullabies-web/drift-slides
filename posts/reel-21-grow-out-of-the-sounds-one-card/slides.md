## Card copy

Headline: **I looked away for one second**

```
and the newborn cry was gone. The small one — the one that sounded like a
kitten in a shoebox.

I looked away and she started answering me. Not words. Just sound. Just her,
deciding to talk back.

I looked away and the little grunt she made falling asleep on my chest stopped
happening,
_and I never got to hear the last one._

Everyone warned me she'd grow out of the clothes.

*Nobody warned me she'd grow out of the sounds.*
```

The closing line is a beat (`"beats": [4]`), so it gets the wider gap on both sides — it is the
payoff the other four paragraphs are building toward, and it earns the air.

## Caption

Rewritten; the published caption retold the poem. The new one names the pattern the card leaves
implicit — every warning a new mother gets is about something she can put in a box, and none of them
are about the things that simply stop. It closes on the one action this lane can honestly ask for:
record thirty seconds of ordinary.
