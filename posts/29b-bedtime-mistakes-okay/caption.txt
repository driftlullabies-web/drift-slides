The bedtime-routine 'mistakes' almost every new mom makes — and why they're okay.

Waiting for them to get 'tired enough'. Skipping the wind-down on the hard nights. A bedtime that drifts later and later. Too much going on right before you put them down. Almost every new mom does some of these while figuring it out — and a 'mistake' doesn't mean you're doing it wrong, it means you learned something new about your little one. There's no perfect routine, only what works for your family. Keep trying things until you find your rhythm.
