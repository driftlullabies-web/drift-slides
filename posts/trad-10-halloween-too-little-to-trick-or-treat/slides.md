## Items
1 Go to the pumpkin patch on a weekday morning. Better light, nobody there.
2 Put them in the cute costume for no reason — it may be the last year you get to choose what they wear.
3 Hand out the candy together. You hold the bowl, they hold the stare.
4 Walk the block with the other trick-or-treaters and knock on nobody's door.
5 One photo beside the pumpkin, same size pumpkin, every year.
