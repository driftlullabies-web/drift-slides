If you're counting your newborn's naps instead of hours, read this.

Newborns don't run on a nap schedule yet. Total sleep across the day matters more than how it's split, four short naps can still be a good day, and watching the awake window beats watching the nap count.

Save this for the next day the naps fall apart. 🤍
