## Items
1 Take the same walk before the cooking starts, every Thanksgiving morning.
2 Ask each guest for one wish for them. Write it in the back of the family cookbook.
3 One photo of every guest holding them, in the same corner of the room each year.
4 Keep the place card with their name on it. Year one, it's just their name.
5 Next year, read last year's wishes out loud before anyone eats.
