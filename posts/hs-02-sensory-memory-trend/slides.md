## Structural note — why slide 7 doesn't break the resolution rule

The format's hard rule is that the resolution lands on the CTA slide. Slide 7 hands her the answer
("they sing the same lullaby every night") three slides early — but slide 7 reopens it in the same
breath ("but here's where it gets really special…"), so the loop closes and reopens on one card and
she still needs slide 10. A double turn, not a leak. Ben agreed to this reading 2026-09-07.

## ⚠️ `check_closers.py` FAILS this post, and the copy was NOT changed

> `[MINE] HS 02` · closer: *"There are plenty of services that will put your baby's name into an
> existing song."* → **LONG (16 words, max 12)**

**The check is applying a carousel rule to a format it was not written for.** The ≤12-word rule
exists for the Follow / Save / Share closing CARD, and its own rationale is that the line should
still make sense pasted onto a different post in the same lane. A hook-stack card has three parts —
**Lead / Ask / Imperative** — and the Lead is a **concession**, structurally the same job as the
website card's eyebrow, which has never been word-capped. It is not a closer and it cannot be
lane-portable: conceding "plenty of services will put a name into an existing song" is the specific
move that earns "Drift writes an entirely new lullaby just for them" on the next line.

**Two reasons it was left alone.** This is copy you reviewed at full 10-slide preview on 2026-09-07
and marked LOCKED, and the run does not quietly reword reviewed material to satisfy a linter. And
the fix that would actually be correct is in the checker — `check_closers.py` should read a `Lead:`
line as a concession and exempt it, the same way the reel CTA fix taught `build_run_sheet.py` about
`card.json`. Changing a checker mid-run so this week passes is the wrong order.

**So: the 4g check exits non-zero on this one row, deliberately, and it is reported rather than
silenced.** HS 01 passed only because its Lead happens to be six words long — not because the rule
fits the format.
